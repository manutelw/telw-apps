Why we exist

Most people don't need more grammar. They need practice and honest feedback.

You may know the rules and the words. But when you have to speak, you pause, you rush, or you lose your point. Lectures can't fix that. Practice can.

So at TELW, you speak in every class. We listen closely. Then we tell you what to change, and you try again.

You speak, not just listen

Most of each class is your talking time. You learn by doing.

Feedback on how you sound

Not only what you say. Your pace, your pauses, your stress and your voice.

Progress you can see

We check your level at the start, track it as you train, and check it again at the end.

How a class works

The same simple cycle, every time

  1. Draw out

    We start with what you already know and say.

  2. Show

    You see a better way to say it, and why it works.

  3. Practise

    You try it out, with support, until it feels easy.

  4. Use

    You use it on your own, in a real task.

  5. Feedback

    You hear what went well and what to fix.

  6. Repeat

    You go again, a little better each time.

Grammar comes up inside the lesson, when you need it. Not as a separate subject.

What TELW is not

So you know exactly what you are signing up for.

  • Not a coaching class. No big batches and no learning by heart.
  • Not grammar lectures. You learn grammar while you speak.
  • Not a school tuition. We don't teach the school syllabus.
  • Not a quick fix. Real change takes practice. We make every class count.
