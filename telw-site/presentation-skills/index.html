Make the point. Hold the room.

For people who need to present an idea, recommendation, report or proposal to an audience.

A strong presentation helps the listener understand what matters and what to do next.

Ask about this programmeAll programmes

What you will work on

Training starts with the situations you face. We focus on the changes that will make your communication useful in those situations.

01

Plan for your audience

Decide what listeners need to know, why they should care, and what you want them to take away.

02

Build a clear structure

Open with purpose, develop the argument, show evidence, and close with a useful next step.

03

Deliver and defend

Use slides as support, speak to people rather than reading, and handle questions with composure.

How the practice works

You prepare a real presentation, deliver it, get feedback on the message and delivery, then revise and present again. We work on the opening, flow, evidence, voice and Q&A.

Who this is for: MBA students, managers, team leaders and client-facing professionals.

In practice

Instead of starting with a list of topics, begin with the decision your audience needs to make and the reason it matters.

From an idea to an audience decision

TELW’s original presentation programme is for anyone who speaks to a group or needs to sell an idea. You learn to make the presentation fit the people in front of you and the decision they face.

  • Identify what the audience already knows, what it needs and what may concern it.
  • Choose a clear point and arrange supporting ideas so the argument builds naturally.
  • Use evidence and examples to make the message convincing rather than merely polished.
  • Manage delivery, visual support and the impact of your voice.
  • Answer difficult questions and handle unexpected situations without losing your purpose.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a presentation from my own work?

Yes. A real presentation helps us work on your audience, message, evidence, delivery and questions.

Does the programme include handling audience questions?

Yes. You practise listening to a question, answering its main point and supporting the answer without losing your flow.

Start with a conversation.

Tell us what you need to do more confidently. We will suggest a suitable starting point after understanding your goal and current level.
