What you will work on

Training starts with the situations you face. We focus on the changes that will make your communication useful in those situations.

01

CV and role fit

Choose evidence that matches a job description, then turn it into a clear, truthful CV and introduction.

02

Group discussions

Enter at the right moment, develop a point with reasons, engage with others and help the group move forward.

03

Personal interviews

Answer with relevant examples, respond to follow-ups and explain why you fit the role.

One connected story

Your CV, discussion points and interview answers should support the same evidence of what you can do. We diagnose the gaps, practise the situations and give targeted feedback.

Who this is for: Students and early-career professionals preparing for internships, campus placements or job changes.

In practice

A candidate may have a strong internship example. We use it in the CV, discuss its business implications in a GD, and explain the candidate’s own contribution in the interview.

Preparation across selection stages

Placement readiness connects the evidence in your profile with the way you speak and write during selection.

  • Read a job description and choose truthful evidence of fit.
  • Build or revise the CV and a short introduction around that evidence.
  • Contribute to group discussions with a point, a reason and a response to others.
  • Answer interview questions with examples and handle follow ups.
  • Write clearly in a selection task, then review what the result shows about your next practice need.
