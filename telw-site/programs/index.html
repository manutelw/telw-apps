TELW Programs

Three ways in: build your everyday English, train for the exact situation your work throws at you, or, in Classes 9 to 12, learn to speak with clarity and confidence.

Every program starts from an assessment of where you already are — not from zero. Pick the track that matches what you actually need right now.

Track 02

Career Development Programs

Domain knowledge only counts if you can put it to work — and that means communicating it well. Pick from six Career Development Skills, each built around a specific situation you actually face at work.

Presentation Skills

For anyone presenting ideas or speaking to groups

Learn to shape a presentation around your audience, structure your argument, and hold the room — including handling tough questions without losing the thread.

More on Presentation Skills →

Negotiation Skills

For anyone negotiating in a business context

The language of proposals and counter-proposals — accepting, rejecting, working through obstacles, and closing the deal in English with control.

More on Negotiation Skills →

Email Writing Skills

For anyone who writes emails or letters at work

Plan and write emails that are clear, direct, and reader-first — drafting real business correspondence, not textbook exercises.

More on Email Writing Skills →

Interview Skills

For anyone preparing for job interviews

The verbal and non-verbal language of presenting yourself well — strengths, weaknesses, composure under pressure, and closing an interview on the right note.

More on Interview Skills →

Telephoning Skills

For receptionists and customer-facing staff

Making and receiving business calls with the right register — handling difficult calls while keeping the customer-care standard high.

More on Telephoning Skills →

Voice Quality Training

For anyone working on pronunciation and clarity

Correct intonation, pitch, stress and fluency — for a thicker accent, cross-border communication, or simply speaking with more presence.

More on Voice Quality Training →
