Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This policy applies to the classes, courses and diagnostics offered by TELW The English Workshop, operated by Manu Vikraman as sole proprietor, New Delhi 110016, India ("TELW").

1. Classes and courses

  • Before the first class: if you cancel before your first class takes place, your course fee is refunded in full.
  • After the course starts: once the first class has taken place, course fees are not refundable.
  • Missed classes: tell us at least 24 hours before a class and we will reschedule it. A class missed without 24 hours' notice counts as taken and is not refunded or rescheduled.
  • Classes cancelled by TELW: if TELW has to cancel a class, it is rescheduled at a time that suits you, at no extra cost.

2. Diagnostics (fluency diagnostic and TELW Level)

  • Once a diagnostic has been started, it is not refundable.
  • TELW Level must be taken within 30 days of payment. After 30 days, access expires and the fee is not refundable.
  • If a technical fault on TELW's side stops your diagnostic from being completed or marked, TELW gives you a fresh attempt instead of a refund.
  • There is no refund because of the level or result you received.

The IELTS diagnostics follow their own IELTS Refund Policy.

3. Duplicate payments

If you were charged twice for the same purchase, the extra payment is refunded in full.

4. How to ask for a refund

Send a WhatsApp message to +91 97171 16831 with your name, the email address used to pay and your Razorpay payment reference.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through Razorpay within 7 working days of approval. Your bank or card provider may take a few more days to show the money in your account.

Grievances

Any grievance should be conveyed by a message on WhatsApp to +91 97171 16831 (messages only, no calls). You can also write to manutelw@gmail.com. We reply within 7 working days.
