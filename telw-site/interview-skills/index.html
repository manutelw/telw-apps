TELW / Interview Skills

Let your evidence do the talking.

For candidates who have experience or ability but struggle to show it clearly in an interview.

We help you explain your fit for a role with specific examples, concise answers and calm follow-up responses.

Ask about this programmeAll programmes

What you will work on

Training starts with the situations you face. We focus on the changes that will make your communication useful in those situations.

01

Tell your story

Introduce your background and direction in a way that fits the job, without reciting your CV.

02

Answer with evidence

Give a direct answer, explain your reason, add a relevant example, and show what it reveals about you.

03

Handle follow-ups

Listen carefully, clarify when needed, and respond to pressure questions without guessing or rambling.

From job description to follow-up

We look at the role and your CV, identify the strongest proof of fit, practise likely questions and follow-ups, and refine your answers aloud. The process also covers the questions you ask the interviewer and your follow-up after the meeting.

Who this is for: Fresh graduates, people changing roles and experienced candidates preparing for a new opportunity.

In practice

“I am a good leader” is a claim. “I brought a divided team to a decision by…” gives an interviewer something to assess.

Preparation starts before the interview

The original TELW course follows the candidate from the decision to apply through the day after the interview. It joins role choice, CV, spoken answers and follow up into one process.

  • Read the job advertisement and decide whether the role fits your strengths and direction.
  • Make the CV an honest, relevant introduction to what you can do.
  • Explain strengths and weaknesses with evidence instead of labels.
  • Use words, voice and body language to make a calm, approachable impression.
  • Listen to questions and remarks, respond clearly, and stay composed under pressure.
  • End the meeting appropriately, ask useful questions and follow up afterwards.

English readiness

The older programme expected a working level of English before specialist interview training. We check your starting point and recommend language work first if an English gap would get in the way of useful interview practice.

Questions people ask

Will I practise answers for a specific role?

You can bring the job description and your CV. We use both to choose relevant evidence and practise likely questions and follow-ups.

What if an interviewer says my answers lack depth?

We look for the missing reason, example, result or reflection. Then you practise giving enough evidence while keeping the answer focused.

Start with a conversation.

Tell us what you need to do more confidently. We will suggest a suitable starting point after understanding your goal and current level.
