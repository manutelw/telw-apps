How the training works

A capacity-building approach, not a coaching class. Every learner follows the same four stages.

  1. Diagnose

    A one-to-one fluency diagnostic identifies your level and your specific blockers.

  2. Place

    You start at the right level, with a bridge course if the skill programme needs one.

  3. Train

    Small-group or one-to-one sessions focused on doing the skill, not hearing about it.

  4. Practise and measure

    Scored practice and feedback between sessions, with reports on progress.

Frequently asked questions

What happens in the ₹499 fluency diagnostic?

A 30-minute one-to-one session. You get your current level with spoken feedback, the two or three habits holding you back, a sample of how the training works, and an improvement plan based on your goal.

Do I need good English before joining a skills programme?

Skills programmes such as presentation, interview and email writing need a working level of English. If the diagnostic shows a gap, you start with a General English bridge course first.

Is training available online?

Yes. Classroom training runs in New Delhi, live sessions run online, and placement practice is available anytime on ClarionPrep.

Do you train groups for colleges and companies?

Yes. Institutional programmes cover B-school placement readiness and workplace communication for teams, with diagnostics and progress reporting for the people who commission the training.

Where do I practise group discussions and interviews?

On ClarionPrep.com, which offers GD practice, PI practice, an AI mock interview, a written communication test and the Placement Readiness Pass.
