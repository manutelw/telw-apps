TELW / Why TELW

Practice that belongs to your life and work.

The TELW approach combines active speaking, regular revisiting and feedback on what people actually say.

Talk to TELWExplore programmes

Start speaking early

Learners use English from the start. Listening, repeating and speaking help a new phrase become something they can use, rather than only recognise.

Keep earlier learning alive

New language builds on older language. Learners revisit expressions and structures across different situations so that progress does not vanish after one lesson.

Use language often

Sessions use role plays, discussion, debate and presentation alongside audio and written work where useful. Learners move, talk and test ideas instead of sitting through a lecture.

Build useful chunks

Practice includes expressions, collocations and other language building blocks. Pronunciation, fluency and accuracy develop alongside the ability to choose words for a particular meaning.

Tailor the work

Training is adapted to the learner and the situations in which they will use English, whether for work, studies, business, travel or everyday life.

Connect speaking with reading and writing

Reading and writing support spoken English. For general English, better spoken control can make writing easier; a specific writing goal receives focused practice of its own.
