Why the two go together

Same student. Same question.

A relative, a professor or an interviewer asksSo, why did you choose this course?
Without a plan, and without practice

Actually, my cousin did it and everyone said it has good scope. So I also thought, why not. Let's see.

With both

I like solving real problems with numbers. My career assessment showed that clearly. This course lets me do that every day, and it leads to the kind of work I actually want.

Knows why

Counselling gives you a real reason for your choice, not a borrowed one.

Says it clearly

Speaking training helps you put that reason into a few clear sentences.

Sounds sure

When the choice is yours and the words come easily, people believe you.

How it works

Four steps. One journey.

You can start at any step. Most families start at the first one.

  1. The Career Roadmap

    Find your path

    A career assessment and one-to-one counselling. You learn your strengths, interests and options, so you choose with a clear head.

  2. The Career Roadmap

    Choose where to study

    India, abroad, or an international course in India. You compare courses, colleges, countries and costs, and pick what fits you, not just what's popular.

  3. TELW

    Learn to speak with confidence

    Speak up in class, explain your ideas and handle interviews. Check your English level or your IELTS before you go.

  4. The Career Roadmap

    Apply and get going

    Help with applications, essays and SOPs, plus scholarships, education loans and visas, so money and paperwork don't hold you back.

Heading abroad? The Career Roadmap also runs French, German, Japanese and Korean courses, so you can start learning the language before you leave.

Questions

What people often ask

Do I have to do every step?

No. You can come only for career counselling, only for speaking training, or for the whole journey. Most families find the steps work best together.

What is a career assessment?

It looks at your interests, strengths, personality and abilities. It shows you which careers are likely to suit you.

Can you help me study abroad?

Yes. The Career Roadmap helps you choose the country, college and course, and guides you through applications, money and visas. TELW helps you get your speaking and English ready.

Why work on speaking as well?

Your choice of college gets you in. How you speak decides how you do in class, in interviews and later at work.

Where do sessions happen?

At our centre in Sector B 5/6, Vasant Kunj, New Delhi. TELW speaking sessions also run online, so you can join from anywhere.
