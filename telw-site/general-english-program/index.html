Speak English with more ease.

For people who understand English but hesitate, translate in their head, or lose their point while speaking.

What you will work on

Training starts with the situations you face. We focus on the changes that will make your communication useful in those situations.

01

Speak with flow

Move from short, uncertain answers to connected thoughts. Practise giving reasons, examples and clear endings.

02

Use language accurately

Work on structures and vocabulary at your level, then use them in conversation rather than memorising lists.

03

Listen and respond

Follow a speaker’s point, ask for clarity, and respond without rehearsing every sentence.

A practical starting point

We check how you speak and understand English now. Your training then uses situations from your work, studies and daily life. You speak regularly, receive specific feedback, and try again.

Who this is for: Students, job seekers and professionals who want a stronger working base in English.

In practice

You know the answer, but the sentence will not come when someone asks. We practise the answer aloud until you can say it naturally.

What the programme develops

The work is level based and grows with the learner. These are the abilities the original TELW General English programme sets out to build.

  • Use structures and vocabulary that suit your present level, then extend your range as you progress.
  • Improve fluency and accuracy together: speak more freely while noticing what needs correction.
  • Pronounce words clearly in your own voice, without trying to copy a foreign accent.
  • Link thoughts in a logical order so a listener can follow a longer answer.
  • Express fine differences in meaning, state your views with confidence and respond to other people.
  • Build listening and reading alongside speaking; use what you understand in your own speech.

A route through the levels

TELW groups the journey as Basic, Intermediate and Advanced. The right starting point comes from a needs analysis, not a guess. At each stage you practise new language in conversation while revisiting what you learnt earlier.

Questions people ask

I understand English. Why do I still hesitate to speak?

Understanding a sentence and building your own answer are different skills. We find where you pause, then practise short answers, connected ideas and real conversations.

Do I need to know my English level before I enquire?

No. We first listen to how you speak and understand English, then suggest a suitable starting point.

Tell us what you need to do more confidently. We will suggest a suitable starting point after understanding your goal and current level.
