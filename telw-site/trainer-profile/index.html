Manu Vikraman

Founder & Senior Consultant, TELW — The English Workshop, New Delhi

  • TESOL-certified communication trainer
  • IELTS examiner experience
  • 22 years in language and communication training

He helps learners organise their thoughts, get to the point and support their answers with reasons and evidence, so that what they know still comes through when the questions get harder.

Experience

Across 22 years, he has worked with school students, undergraduates, MBA candidates and working professionals. He spent 12 years as faculty with inlingua, beginning in 2004, before founding TELW in Vasant Kunj in 2016.

His learners have included people in banking, technology, outsourcing, manufacturing, publishing, education, hospitality, energy, financial services, real estate, NGOs and legal work, as well as learners from many countries. Before his training career, he worked in English and Hindi translation and interpretation, including literary, commercial and legal material.

How he trains

His sessions centre on practice. Learners speak, discuss, present and respond to questions, then receive clear feedback on what worked, what was missing and how to improve. Digital speaking-practice and diagnostic tools he has built himself extend that practice between sessions.

Who he trains

Students

For students in Classes 9–12 and college, Manu teaches clear speaking, organised thinking and thoughtful answers without memorised scripts. His CEFR-based syllabus lets each learner start at their actual level and build step by step. The focus is language and communication, rather than school-subject tuition or personal etiquette. IELTS candidates learn from a trainer with experience as an IELTS examiner.

Students of Professional Educational Institutions

Manu designs communication and placement-readiness programmes covering interviews, group discussions, presentations and professional communication. In 2026, as Senior Consultant — Learning & Development at FIIB, New Delhi, he prepared management students for interviews and specific recruitment opportunities, working with programme and placement teams.

Working professionals

TELW offers focused training in presentations, meetings, business writing and handling difficult questions. Manu has also taught General English, Business English, negotiation, telephone and social communication, and voice quality.

Adult learners

Manu also teaches adults who want better English for everyday life: homemakers, retired people, and people from any country who live in India and want to use English with confidence, whatever their first language. Through General English, they build confidence in conversation, phone calls and dealings with schools, offices and neighbours, starting from their own level.

Start with a diagnostic

Every engagement starts with a diagnostic. How does the learner or team communicate now? Where do they lose impact? What should they work on first?

Have an interview, a presentation or a team that needs to communicate better? Speak to TELW about a diagnostic and a focused training plan.
