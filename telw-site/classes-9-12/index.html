For parents of students in Classes 9 to 12

Good marks in English. One-word answers at the dinner table.

Your child has ideas worth hearing. We help them express those ideas clearly, join discussions and speak with greater ease and confidence. This is purely communication skills training, on our own CEFR-based syllabus. We don't teach school subjects.

What you may be noticing

You've heard your child speak. You know something is missing.

School rewards what a student can write down. Very little of it tests what they can say out loud, in their own words, with someone looking at them. These are the gaps that show.

Fluency

The words don't come

  • Stops mid-sentence, hunting for the next word
  • Knows the answer in class and still won't raise a hand
  • Chats easily with friends, freezes in English in front of adults
  • Fills every gap with "umm", "like" and "basically"
Intelligence

The thinking doesn't show

  • Bright on paper, ordinary out loud
  • Offers an opinion and has no reason behind it
  • Repeats what the textbook or the internet said
  • Goes blank at the follow-up question: "Why?"
Depth

The answer runs out

  • Says everything in one line, then has nothing left
  • Can't stay on a topic for two minutes
  • Sees one side of a question and stops there
  • Speaks in the short phrases of reels and chat messages
Personality

The child holds back

  • Avoids debates, the stage and any chance to lead
  • Goes quiet in front of teachers and relatives
  • Looks down, and the voice drops, when spoken to
  • Gives in the moment someone disagrees

Intelligently

Let us be clear

We are not a finishing school.

Many personality development courses stop at appearance and etiquette. A child can master all of it and still have nothing to say when someone asks what they think.

You won't find this here
  • School subjects and exam tuition
  • Knife in the right hand, fork in the left
  • How to dress for the occasion
  • Table manners and dining etiquette
  • Grooming, hair and make-up
  • How to walk, sit and stand
  • Memorised lines that sound impressive
What personality means to us

It is what people notice when your child speaks. That comes from having something to say and the nerve to say it.

  • Looks people in the eye while talking
  • Speaks to an adult without shrinking
  • Holds a view when someone challenges it
  • Disagrees without being rude
  • Says "I don't know" and recovers
  • Volunteers, instead of waiting to be picked
Why a separate programme

A 15-year-old is not a small adult.

Our adult programmes prepare people for interviews, meetings and client calls. Classes for school students are suited to your child's age, level and goals.

Scope of the training

Communication skills only, on a CEFR-based syllabus

TELW has its own syllabus and teaching method, based on the CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages) courseline. It runs independently of your child's school and board.

What this is

  • Purely communication skills training
  • TELW's own syllabus and teaching method
  • Based on the CEFR courseline

What this is not

  • Tuition in school subjects
  • Preparation for school or board exams
  • Coverage of the school English textbook
In class

Your child will spend much of the class speaking and listening.

What your child will practise
  • Giving fuller, clearer answers
  • Putting thoughts in an order that others can follow
  • Speaking with a clear voice and steady pace
  • Sharing views, listening to others and taking turns
  • Answering fresh questions without a script
How they learn

The trainer gives clear feedback and time to try again. Students learn to use words and sentences correctly through speaking.

Is your child very shy?

Then this was built with them in mind. We design every batch around the student who needs the most support, so the quiet ones are never left behind by the confident ones.

How you will see progress

We measure your child's progress against their own starting point.

Your child also gets guidance for practice at home, and we explain how you can help.

At the start

We agree on clear goals with you.

During the course

We share what your child does well and where they need more practice.

At the end

We review what has changed and suggest the next step.
