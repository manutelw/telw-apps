TELW / GD & PI Skills

Hold your own in the room.

For candidates facing a group discussion or personal interview, whether for a job or another selection round.

We help you build an argument, listen and respond to others, and answer follow-up questions without memorised scripts.

Ask about this programmeAll programmes

What you will work on

Training starts with the selection round you are preparing for. We focus on the changes that will make the most difference in that round.

01

Contribute to the discussion

Enter at the right moment, make a point with a reason, and respond to what others have said.

02

Move the discussion forward

Listen closely, build on a good point, question a weak one and help the group get somewhere useful.

03

Answer in the interview

Explain your background, choices and views with relevant examples, then stay composed through the follow-up.

Practice that feels like the real round

You take part in group discussions and mock interviews, then get feedback on what worked, what was missing and what to do differently in the next attempt.

Who this is for: Job candidates, college students and students of professional educational institutions preparing for a selection round.

In practice

“I agree with him” adds nothing. “I agree, but we have not looked at the cost” gives the group something to discuss.

English readiness

We check your starting point and recommend language work first if an English gap would get in the way of useful GD and PI practice.

Questions people ask

Is this only for job placements?

No. The same skills apply to any selection round that uses a group discussion or personal interview.

How is this different from Job Placement Skills?

This programme focuses on the group discussion and the personal interview. Job Placement Skills also covers your CV, role fit and written tasks.

Start with a conversation.

Tell us which selection round you are preparing for. We will suggest a suitable starting point after understanding your goal and current level.
