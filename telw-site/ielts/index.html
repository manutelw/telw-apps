Questions people ask

Are these official IELTS tests?

No. These are original TELW diagnostics. They are not official IELTS examinations or calibrated band predictions, and TELW is not affiliated with the IELTS test owners.

Which test type should I choose?

Choose the type you plan to take. Reading has separate Academic and General Training versions. Listening is the same for both.
