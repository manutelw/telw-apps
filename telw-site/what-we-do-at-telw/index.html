TELW / How TELW Teaches

Learn to use English by using it.

TELW begins with your needs, gives you room to speak and helps you make each message clearer.

Talk to TELWExplore programmes

Needs before lessons

We look at both what you can understand and what you can use. We ask what you want to do in English, then choose practice that matches your starting point and goal.

Speaking, listening and reading together

Learners listen, speak and read in connected tasks. For general English, stronger speaking can support writing. When the goal is a specific written skill such as professional email or report writing, TELW teaches it directly.

Participation from the beginning

People speak with one another, use language in role plays and try it again after feedback. This reduces hesitation. The classroom aims to feel friendly and active, rather than like a school lecture.

Think and speak in English

The method discourages translating each sentence from another language. Learners build an idea, describe the picture in their minds and find a natural English way to express it.

Discover how the language works

The general English class does not rely on long grammar explanations or memorised rules. Learners use meaningful examples, notice patterns and receive guidance when something needs correction.

What can improve

The intended gains include accuracy, fluency, vocabulary, nuance, comfort and confidence. The point is to express a thought clearly and assertively in a real conversation.
