E-Portfolios

Show your proof

What it is

A guided way to turn your CV, projects, achievements and experience into a short visual portfolio that shows what you have actually done.

Advantages

  • Makes evidence easier to see than in a CV alone.
  • Helps recruiters understand your work quickly.
  • Supports interviews, networking and internship applications.

Who can use it

Students, fresh graduates, job seekers and professionals who have projects, internships, achievements or work samples worth showing.

How to use it

Start with your CV and proof of work, choose the strongest evidence, build the portfolio, review the flow and then share it as part of your professional profile.

Résumé

Position your evidence

What it is

Résumé support focused on clarity, relevance, evidence and positioning—not cosmetic formatting alone.

Advantages

  • Makes strengths and results easier to identify.
  • Reduces vague or unsupported claims.
  • Improves readiness for CV-based interview questions.

Who can use it

Students, freshers and professionals preparing for internships, placements, job changes or role-specific applications.

How to use it

Begin with your current résumé and target role. Review each section for relevance and proof, correct weak claims, then prepare to defend the final version in an interview.

JD Builder

Understand the role

What it is

A tool that converts a long or confusing Job Description into a clear preparation brief: responsibilities, capabilities, priorities and likely selection signals.

Advantages

  • Shows what the employer is really asking for.
  • Separates essential requirements from background noise.
  • Creates a sharper base for CV and interview preparation.

Who can use it

Anyone preparing for a specific internship, placement drive, graduate role or job application.

How to use it

Paste the JD, review the structured brief, identify the most important capabilities and use those priorities to guide the rest of your preparation.

JD Mapper

Connect role to evidence

What it is

A role-fit tool that maps the requirements in a JD against what your CV and experience can genuinely support.

Advantages

  • Identifies strong and weak matches.
  • Shows where interviewers may probe.
  • Helps you find evidence before the interview.

Who can use it

Candidates who already have a target JD and want role-specific preparation rather than generic interview practice.

How to use it

Provide the JD and your CV/profile, review the capability mapping, identify evidence for each important requirement and prepare responses around the gaps and strong matches.

See JD Mapper →

CV Builder & Evaluator

Build and assess

What it is

A CV-quality tool that evaluates the strength of a CV, identifies weaknesses and helps improve content for a target role where a JD is available.

Advantages

  • Provides a structured quality check.
  • Highlights weak evidence and unclear wording.
  • Can improve role alignment before submission.

Who can use it

Students, freshers and professionals who need to create, review or strengthen a CV before applying.

How to use it

Paste your CV, add the JD when preparing for a specific role, review the evaluation and correction points, then revise and re-check the document.

PCL

Build the skill

What it is

A structured Professional Communication & Leadership learning programme delivered through short modules and guided practice.

Advantages

  • Builds the underlying skill before high-stakes performance.
  • Breaks communication into manageable capabilities.
  • Allows learners to progress module by module.

Who can use it

Students, early-career professionals and learners who need systematic communication development rather than only interview rehearsal.

How to use it

Work through the modules in sequence, complete the practice within each module and apply the learning later in ASCENT, interviews, presentations and workplace situations.

Open PCL →

Live Mock Interview

Perform under pressure

What it is

A spoken interview simulation that asks questions, follows up and gives the learner a more realistic test of interview performance.

Advantages

  • Tests performance rather than preparation notes.
  • Creates pressure and unpredictability closer to a real interview.
  • Shows what still breaks down when the learner must answer live.

Who can use it

Candidates who have prepared the role and their evidence and now need a realistic rehearsal before an actual interview.

How to use it

Choose or enter the interview context, answer the questions orally, respond to follow-ups and review the feedback after the simulation.
