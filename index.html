About ASCENT

Practice that turns into visible performance.

ASCENT is not a content library. It is a practice-and-correction system built around actual spoken responses.

🎙

Record real responses

Students answer assigned or self-selected questions in their own voice, under a clear response time.

✓

Receive criterion feedback

Responses are scored using the chosen PI, GD or Managerial Communication rubric.

↗

Track improvement

First and latest scores, attempts, criterion results and task completion create a visible progress record.

▦

Support institutional reporting

Trainers can assign questions, review participation, use dashboards and export structured reports.

How it works

A simple practice loop.

Each cycle moves the learner from a question to a clearer, more professional response.

Receive or choose a task

The trainer assigns a formal task, or the student uses available practice.

Plan and record

The question, response time and selected rubric are clear before recording begins.

Review the result

ASCENT returns a score, criterion feedback and an exact improvement action.

Try again with purpose

The student improves the weak line, strengthens the evidence and records a better response.

For students

Know what to practise next.

ASCENT keeps the work visible and manageable instead of leaving students with vague advice such as “communicate better.”

  • See assigned questions before recording.
  • Use PI, GD and Managerial Communication practice.
  • Receive clear, specific feedback on each response.
  • Review completed work and attempt history.
  • Build confidence through repeated spoken practice.
For trainers & institutions

Assign, track and report without manual chasing.

The trainer portal brings question banks, assignments, results, participation and reporting into one operational view.

  • Create and assign formal speaking tasks.
  • Select from organised LUM, PI and GD question banks.
  • Control rubrics, follow-ups, release pace and attempts.
  • See participation, scores and student improvement.
  • Download Excel reports for institutional review.

Make spoken practice visible, measurable and repeatable.

ASCENT gives learners a place to practise and gives trainers the evidence needed to guide progress.
