The five apps

Choose the tool that matches what you need to do now.

The apps remain separate, so you can use them individually. They also work as a connected preparation system when you need more than one.

A
Spoken Practice

ASCENT

Practise how you speak, answer and respond.

ASCENT is an oral-response practice system for LUM, Personal Interviews, Group Discussions, Diagnostic and Custom questions. Learners record their answers, receive structured feedback and use that feedback to improve the next response.

Use ASCENT when: you need repeated spoken practice, clearer answers, stronger evidence, better response structure, interview practice or discussion practice.
Open ASCENT →
JM
Role Fit

JD Mapper

Map a specific job to your own CV and experience.

JD Mapper compares what the role asks for with what the learner can actually show. It helps identify strong matches, weak matches, missing evidence and the areas an interviewer is most likely to probe.

Use JD Mapper when: you are preparing for a particular company or role and need to know what in your profile supports the JD—and what you may have to defend.
Open JD Mapper →
JB
JD Analysis

JD Builder

Turn a long Job Description into a usable preparation brief.

JD Builder breaks down a Job Description into the responsibilities, capabilities, expectations and signals that matter. It helps the learner see what the employer is really looking for before interview preparation begins.

Use JD Builder when: you have received a JD and need to understand the role, the priorities behind it and the capabilities likely to matter in selection.
Open JD Builder →
PCL
Learning

PCL

Build the professional communication skills behind good performance.

PCL is a structured learning programme made up of communication modules. It develops the underlying habits, judgement and communication capability learners need before they apply those skills in interviews and workplace situations.

Use PCL when: you need to learn or strengthen the skill itself—not merely practise an interview answer.
Open PCL →
MI
Simulation

Live Mock Interview

Test how well your preparation holds up in a real conversation.

Live Mock Interview creates a spoken interview experience with questions and follow-ups. It is designed to test whether the learner can think, respond and communicate effectively when the conversation is moving in real time.

Use Live Mock Interview when: you have prepared and practised and now want to test your readiness under realistic interview conditions.
Open Live Mock Interview →
