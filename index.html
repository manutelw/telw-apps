Labs, practice & live services

Know what each service does before you ask for access.

Institutional access is controlled by the relevant trainer or administrator. Private candidates can request access by emailing the administrator at manutelw@gmail.com.

GD Lab

What it is: a trainer-released, JD-linked group-discussion lab built around the company, role and discussion context.

Benefits: focused preparation, realistic discussion exposure and clearer role-relevant participation.

Who can use it: institutional learners specifically assigned the lab by a trainer, and private candidates given access by the administrator.

Access: institutional learners cannot self-apply. The lab appears only when the trainer gives/release access.

GD Practice

What it is: structured practice for entering, responding, developing, guiding and closing a group discussion.

Benefits: repeated speaking practice, stronger GD moves and better control of contribution.

Who can use it: institutional learners whose access application is approved, and private candidates granted access.

How to apply: sign in to ASCENT, open Practice Access and submit a GD Practice request.

Apply for GD Practice access →

PI Lab

What it is: a trainer-released, JD-specific personal-interview preparation lab built around the actual company and role.

Benefits: targeted questions, role-specific preparation and more relevant spoken responses.

Who can use it: institutional learners specifically included by the trainer, and private candidates given access by the administrator.

Access: institutional learners cannot self-apply. The lab is available only when a trainer releases it to them.

PI Practice

What it is: repeated practice of personal-interview questions with recorded spoken responses.

Benefits: clearer answers, stronger evidence and greater interview fluency under time pressure.

Who can use it: institutional learners whose access application is approved, and private candidates granted access.

How to apply: sign in to ASCENT, open Practice Access and submit a PI Practice request.

Apply for PI Practice access →

Dialogue Lab

What it is: guided listening and spoken-dialogue practice designed to build usable communication through repeated models and learner responses.

Benefits: more natural spoken language, better listening-to-response control and repeated practice at the learner's level.

Who can use it: institutional learners given access by their programme/trainer and private candidates granted access by the administrator.

Access: use the access provided by the trainer/administrator. Private candidates can request access by email.

PCL

What it is: Professional Communication Lab training for workplace and managerial communication.

Benefits: stronger professional speaking, clearer structuring and more effective workplace responses.

Who can use it: institutional learners for whom the lab is released and private candidates granted access by the administrator.

Access: institutional access is provided by the trainer/administrator; private candidates can request access by email.

Live Mock Interview

What it is: a realistic spoken mock interview with live questioning, follow-ups and performance review.

Benefits: pressure practice, stronger response control and a clearer view of interview readiness.

Who can use it: institutional learners scheduled or granted access and private candidates given access by the administrator.

Access: access is controlled by the trainer/administrator. Private candidates can request access by email.

Private candidates: You can request access to any of the services above by emailing the administrator at manutelw@gmail.com. State the service you want to access in your email.
