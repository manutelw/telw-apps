1. Learn through the workplace task

Start with the real question. Build the response before you study the complete model.

Guided practice

Real workplace task

Manager: The customer cannot complete the normal verification. What can we do?

Anticipate your response: What would you say first? Think or say it aloud before continuing.

Language focus

Assess Modals for rules, options and discretion. State the fixed must/must not first. Then use may, can or should for the options inside the boundary. Never present discretion as permission to break the rule. Judge general B1 accuracy only where it affects this task. Do not force the target form where another form fits better. Give one exact correction or model when useful.

Notice it in context: We must not skip identity verification, even when the customer is in a hurry. We can offer the approved video-verification route, and we may give extra time if accessibility is the issue

Build the response

Direct AnswerAnswer the task clearly. WHAT?
ExplainGive the reason. WHY?
Business LinkAdd evidence. PROOF?
Business LinkShow impact. SO WHAT?
JudgementDecide the next step. WHAT NEXT?

TELW remains the response structure. These questions are the managerial thinking inside it.

Put it together: model response

Manager: The customer cannot complete the normal verification. What can we do?
Learner: We must not skip identity verification, even when the customer is in a hurry. We can offer the approved video-verification route, and we may give extra time if accessibility is the issue. This protects the account while still helping the customer. If neither approved route works, we should document the exception and seek a compliance decision.
