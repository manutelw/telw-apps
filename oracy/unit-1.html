English in My World

Three short lessons. Three listening passages. Five questions in each Key Point. Five speaking tasks. Keep your answers simple and clear.

Key Point 1 · Vocabulary

Talking about languages around you

native languageofficial languageat leastalmostmajority

Listen: A busy food fair

Mira: This fair is amazing. I can hear so many languages.

Ravi: Yes. At least six languages are being spoken here today.

Mira: Hindi is my native language, but I use English with many visitors.

Ravi: Same here. English is an official language in many places, so almost everyone knows a little.

Mira: The majority of the signs here are in English and Hindi.

Key Point 3 · Pronunciation

/w/ and /v/: keep the sounds apart

/w/

work · world · word · weekend

Round your lips slightly.

/v/

very · visit · video · travel

Top teeth lightly touch the lower lip.

Listen: A short presentation

I work from home, but I visit my sister every weekend. We watch videos together. I want better English because I would like to travel and speak to people from different parts of the world.
