English in My World

Three short lessons. Three listening passages. Five speaking tasks. Keep your answers simple and clear.

Key Point 1 · Vocabulary

Talking about languages around you

native languageofficial languageat leastalmostmajority

Listen: A busy food fair

Mira: This fair is amazing. I can hear so many languages.

Ravi: Yes. At least six languages are being spoken here today.

Mira: Hindi is my native language, but I use English with many visitors.

Ravi: Same here. English is an official language in many places, so almost everyone knows a little.

Mira: The majority of the signs here are in English and Hindi.

Multiple choice

How many languages are being spoken at the fair?

Linking

Match the word with the meaning.

native language → the first language you learn at home

majority → most of a group

Speaking 1

Say three true sentences about languages around you.

Use any three new words or phrases.

Speaking 2 · Mini monologue

What languages do people use in your home, building, street or town?

Speak for 20–30 seconds.

Key Point 2 · Grammar

Explaining purpose

I use … for -ingI need … to …I’m learning … to …so that I can …

Listen: Why I am learning English

I use English for reading messages and watching videos. I need English to speak to my daughter’s teachers. I am also learning it to travel more easily. I practise for twenty minutes every day so that I can speak with less fear.

Audio-gapped text

I use English reading messages. I need English speak to my daughter’s teachers.

Word order

English / so that / I / practise / I can / speak clearly

Speaking 3

What do you use English for? Why do you need it? Why are you learning it?

Speaking 4 · Dialogue

Coach: Why are you learning English?

Give one reason. Then add: so that I can …

Key Point 3 · Pronunciation

/w/ and /v/: keep the sounds apart

/w/

work · world · word · weekend

Round your lips slightly.

/v/

very · visit · video · travel

Top teeth lightly touch the lower lip.

Listen: A short presentation

I work from home, but I visit my sister every weekend. We watch videos together. I want better English because I would like to travel and speak to people from different parts of the world.

Multiple choice audio

Which word begins with /v/?

Speaking 5 · Final production

Tell us about English in your life.

Speak for 35–50 seconds. Talk about languages around you, what you use English for, and why you are learning it.
