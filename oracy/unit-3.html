Key Point 2 · Grammar

If the weather changes, what will we do?

If it rains, we’ll…It might…It will probably…We won’t…
Notice the patterns.
Real condition: “If it rains, we’ll use the covered garden.”
Prediction: “It might rain.” · “It will probably clear up.”
