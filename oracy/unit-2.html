Speaking 1 · Repeat and vary

Repeat it. Then change umbrella to an object you sometimes need.

Speaking 2 · Personal answer

What do you sometimes borrow from a friend or neighbour?

Say what it is, why you need it, and when you return it.

Key Point 2 · Grammar

Ask without sounding bossy

May I…?Could you…?Would you…?Do you mind if I…?…, isn’t it?
Notice the pattern.
“Open the window.” → “Could you open the window, please?”
“I want this chair.” → “Do you mind if I use this chair?”

Key Point 3 · Pronunciation

Hear the two th sounds

/θ/ — no voice

think · three · thank · something

Let a little air pass over your tongue.

/ð/ — voice on

this · that · they · together

Use the same tongue position. Add your voice.
