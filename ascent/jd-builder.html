How JD Builder works

The app takes one job-description file and turns it into a structured, reviewable report.

1

Upload the complete JD

Use the full source file rather than copying only the attractive parts. JD Builder accepts common document formats and can also take optional context that was shared outside the JD.

Let the JD drive the analysis

The role is broken down before the company research is added. That keeps the briefing anchored to the actual opportunity rather than turning it into a broad company profile.

3

Review the report by section

The output is split into Overview, JD Analysis, Company Research, Student Briefing, and Sources & Checks. That makes it easier to validate each layer before the material is shared.
