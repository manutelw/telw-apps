1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how ASCENT collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal data when you register for, access or use ASCENT, including the website, mobile app, practice systems, assessments, audio features, ASCENT Coach, reports and related services.

2. Data we may process

  • identity and profile data such as name, email address, mobile number, learner or student ID, roll number, institution, batch and academic year;
  • account and authentication data such as session identifiers, login status and access entitlement;
  • practice and learning data such as assigned questions, answers, submissions, attempt history, scores, rubric results, feedback, category performance and progress;
  • audio and speech data where you record responses, including recordings, transcripts and speech-analysis outputs;
  • ASCENT Coach text during the active session, including what you type and the responses shown to you;
  • payment and entitlement information such as plan, purchase channel, subscription status and access period; full card or banking credentials are handled by the relevant payment provider and should not be entered into ASCENT Coach;
  • technical and usage data such as device/browser information, timestamps, error logs, security events and feature usage needed to operate and secure the service;
  • limited misuse-enforcement data such as warning count, incident type, suspension status and date, and where reasonably necessary for review, a limited excerpt of an offending request.

3. Why we use data

ASCENT may process personal data to create and secure accounts; verify access and entitlements; deliver assigned practice and assessments; record submissions and progress; transcribe and evaluate learner responses; generate scores, feedback and recommendations; provide ASCENT Coach and other AI-assisted features; send service, assignment or reminder communications; provide authorised trainer or institutional reports; prevent fraud, misuse and unauthorised access; provide support and grievance handling; comply with law and platform requirements; and maintain and improve reliability, safety and performance.

4. ASCENT Coach conversations

ASCENT does not provide permanent learner chat history for ASCENT Coach. The current conversation may be held temporarily in the learner's browser/session so that the Coach can understand immediate follow-up questions. When the session ends or the page is left or reloaded, the learner should not expect the conversation to remain available.

Learners may use the Download Conversation or copy function to save the visible current conversation before leaving. ASCENT does not intentionally create a permanent full-text Coach transcript for later learner access.

Limited technical, security, usage or misuse-enforcement records may nevertheless be retained where reasonably necessary. Serious misuse review may require retention of a limited excerpt of the offending request rather than an ordinary full conversation history.

5. Service providers

ASCENT uses third-party service providers for parts of the service. Current infrastructure may include Supabase for database/backend functions, Cloudflare for web delivery/hosting, OpenAI for certain AI-assisted functions, Google/Google Play for app distribution and billing where applicable, and email or payment service providers used for particular functions. Data is provided only as reasonably necessary for the relevant service function. ASCENT does not sell learner personal data to advertisers.

6. Institutional learners

If your access is provided through an institution, selected data such as registration status, assigned work, submissions, scores, feedback, completion status and progress may be available to authorised trainers or institutional personnel for legitimate programme administration and learning purposes. Institutional arrangements may also require retention or archiving of particular submissions.

7. Security

ASCENT uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the service, including controlled access, authenticated sessions, restricted privileged functions and secure third-party infrastructure. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their credentials and devices.

8. Retention

Different categories are retained for different periods according to purpose. Ordinary Coach conversations are not maintained as permanent learner chat history. Account, entitlement, submission, score, institutional reporting, security, payment-reference and dispute records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for service operation, contractual or institutional obligations, accounting, security, legal compliance or dispute resolution. Where information is no longer required and no lawful reason requires retention, ASCENT may delete or anonymise it.

9. Account and data deletion

Users may request deletion through the Account & Data Deletion page or by emailing manu@telw.co.in from the email address associated with the account. Deletion may not include information ASCENT is required or permitted to retain for legal compliance, fraud prevention, accounting/payment records, active disputes, security incidents, institutional record obligations or other lawful purposes.

10. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law and identity verification, you may contact ASCENT to request access to information about your data, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, grievance handling or other rights available under applicable law. Requests should be sent to manu@telw.co.in.

11. Do not submit sensitive/confidential information

Do not enter passwords, OTPs, full payment-card or banking credentials, confidential third-party personal data, trade secrets, proprietary employer or client material, confidential placement or internship documents, or information you are not authorised to disclose into free-text or Coach fields unless specifically requested for a lawful ASCENT function.

12. Intended audience

ASCENT is intended primarily for adult learners and career-readiness users. If ASCENT is offered to minors in the future, appropriate consent and safeguards will be implemented as required by applicable law and platform policy.

13. International processing

Some service providers may process data using infrastructure located outside the user's state or country, subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

14. Changes

ASCENT may update this Privacy Policy because of product, legal, security or platform changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated where appropriate.
