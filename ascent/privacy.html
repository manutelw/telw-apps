ASCENT Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 August 2026

ASCENT is a professional communication-practice service operated by Manu Vikraman under TELW The English Workshop. This policy explains what information ASCENT handles, why it is needed and what choices users have.

Information we collect

Depending on the type of access, ASCENT may collect a learner's name, email address, mobile number, student or learner identifier, institution, batch, login information and account status. It also handles assigned questions, audio responses, transcripts, scores, feedback, attempts and progress records.

Technical information needed for security and operation may include browser or device details, login and activity timestamps, network information and error records. The Android app requests microphone access only when the learner chooses to record a response.

How we use information

Information is used to create and secure accounts, provide assigned or self-selected practice, record and evaluate responses, generate feedback, show progress, enforce attempt rules, support learners, produce authorised institutional reports and maintain the reliability and security of ASCENT.

Audio, transcripts and feedback

Audio responses may be transcribed and processed by automated evaluation services to provide communication feedback. Institutional responses may also be made available to authorised trainers or archived for the relevant institution under its programme arrangements. ASCENT evaluates communication performance and does not guarantee employment or selection.

Service providers and authorised access

ASCENT uses contracted technology providers for hosting, databases, authentication, storage, email delivery, automated transcription or evaluation and, on the separate private-purchase website, payment processing. These providers process information only as needed to deliver their services. Authorised trainers and institutions can access information relevant to the learners and programmes they manage.

Payments

The Google Play version of ASCENT does not collect card or bank details. Where a private learner purchases access through the separate ASCENT website, payment information is handled by the payment provider. ASCENT receives transaction references and access-status information rather than full card details.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide ASCENT, maintain programme and assessment records, meet institutional commitments, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations. Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data through the ASCENT account-deletion page. Some limited records may be retained where legally or contractually required, and the user will be told when this applies.

Security

ASCENT uses access controls, encrypted network connections and role-based permissions intended to protect learner information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep their passwords private and report suspected unauthorised access promptly.

Age and intended audience

ASCENT is intended for adult learners and professionals. It is not designed for children.

Your choices

Users may ask to access, correct or delete their personal information. Microphone permission can be withdrawn through the device settings, although spoken-response practice will then not function.

Contact

For privacy questions, corrections or complaints, email manutelw@gmail.com.
