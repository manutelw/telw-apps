How to use it

Paste the complete Job Description and your current CV. JD Mapper extracts the employer’s requirements, then asks you to review each one as Strong, Transferable or None. You remain in control and can correct the suggested rating before questions are built.

2

The questions available

Your interview set covers Introduction, Behavioural, Industry, Situational and Curveball questions. When the JD calls for them, JD Mapper can also add Role/Technical, Values & Culture, Commercial Judgement and Data/Tools questions.

3

How to practise the questions

Select Build My Questions, then Create my speaking practice. ASCENT moves the JD-specific questions into its spoken-practice flow. A quick microphone check runs first, then you record, review feedback and retry rather than simply reading a list.

4

Frameworks to follow

A useful default is TELW: Tell → Engage/Explain → Link → Wrap. Give the answer, support it, connect it to the JD and close clearly. The framework helps structure thinking; it is not a script to memorise.

6

Why being JD-specific matters

A generic bank asks what candidates are usually asked. JD Mapper asks what this employer may need to test for this role. It links the JD requirement to your evidence fit, then turns that combination into targeted interview preparation.
