Step 1 · Paste the JD and your CV

Use the complete text. Do not shorten the JD to only the points you like.

ASCENT keeps distinct requirements separate. A long JD may correctly produce 20–25 or more items.
Use your real current CV. Do not add experience that is not already true.

Step 2 · Review ASCENT’s JD-to-CV map

ASCENT has suggested S, T or N from your pasted CV. Check every item. Change any rating or evidence that does not match your real experience. S is Strong: your CV has direct proof. T is Transferable: your CV has related evidence that can transfer to this role. N is None: there is no clear proof in your CV.

How Evidence Fit is calculated: CORE S = 3, T = 2, N = 0. SECONDARY S = 1, T = 0.5, N = 0. The result is converted to 0–100. Strong requires at least 75/100 and no more than 1 CORE N gap; 2 or more CORE N gaps cap the band at Moderate. It is a preparation indicator, not a selection probability.

Step 3 · Your interview set

Your set includes Introduction, Behavioural, Industry, Situational and Curveball questions. ASCENT adds Role/Technical, Values & Culture, Commercial Judgement or Data/Tools questions only when this JD calls for them.
