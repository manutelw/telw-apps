ASCENT Refund Policy

Effective date: 25 August 2026

ASCENT is operated by Manu Vikraman, sole proprietor of TELW The English Workshop. Refund/support contact: manu@telw.co.in.

1. General rule

ASCENT provides time-limited digital access, learning content, practice systems and AI-assisted services. Except where required by applicable law, the applicable payment platform, or an express written ASCENT offer, fees for activated digital access are generally non-refundable.

2. Situations that ordinarily do not create a refund right

A refund is not ordinarily due merely because a learner did not use the service, used only part of the service, did not complete assigned work, changed their mind after access was activated, failed to use the service during the permitted access period, did not obtain the result expected, or lost Coach access because of the learner's own repeated or serious misuse.

3. Google Play purchases

Purchases made through Google Play are also subject to Google Play's applicable refund procedures and mandatory rules. Google may process a refund in circumstances permitted by its policies. Where Google refers a refund request to ASCENT, ASCENT will review it under this Policy and applicable law.

4. Technical failure

If a material technical failure attributable to ASCENT substantially prevents use of a paid entitlement and ASCENT cannot provide a reasonable correction, extension, replacement access or other appropriate remedy, a refund may be considered or provided where required by law.

5. Duplicate or unauthorised charges

Suspected duplicate charges, billing errors or unauthorised transactions should be reported promptly to the relevant payment platform/provider and to manu@telw.co.in. Additional verification may be required.

6. Misuse and suspension

Temporary or permanent restriction arising from a learner's material breach, fraud, account sharing, security abuse or repeated deliberate misuse does not ordinarily create a right to refund. This does not limit any refund right that applicable law or the payment platform requires.

7. How to request review

Email manu@telw.co.in from the email associated with the ASCENT account. Include the learner name, purchase date, purchase channel, transaction/order reference where available, and a short explanation of the issue.

8. Mandatory rights preserved

Nothing in this Policy excludes or limits consumer rights, statutory remedies or payment-platform refund rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
