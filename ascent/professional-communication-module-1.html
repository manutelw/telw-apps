Module 1

Giving a Manager an Update

Learn how to give a short, useful workplace update — clearly, calmly and with good judgement. You will hear the skill, practise it, then take a fresh test.

2 · Practise

Train

First understand what your manager needs from an update. Then hear a weak version and a stronger version.

Show me

A useful update answers four questions

  • Where are we now?
  • What is the main issue or risk?
  • What have you done about it?
  • What happens next — or what do you need?

What your manager does not need

  • A long history of everything that happened
  • Blame before ownership
  • Vague reassurance such as “I am working on it”
  • A problem with no next step
Weak update

“There has been a problem with the report because I was waiting for some information from another team. I am following up and I hope it should be done soon.”

Stronger update

“The report is one day behind because the final data came in late. I have completed the sections I can finish and followed up for the missing figures. I expect to send the full report by tomorrow afternoon. I’ll flag it straight away if that changes.”

Voice focus · London English

Notice the stress change in update: an UP-date (noun), but to up-DATE (verb). Keep it natural — no exaggerated RP.

Practise

You may retry. ASCENT will coach one useful improvement after each attempt.

Help me practise

Your situation

Your part of a client report is one day late because you received key figures later than expected. You have completed the sections that do not need those figures. Give your manager a short update.

Keep it useful and brief. Aim for about 30–45 seconds.
00:00

Test

Fresh situation. No coaching before you answer. You get one test attempt in this pilot session.

Test me

Your situation

A client presentation is due tomorrow morning. Another team has still not sent one set of figures you need. The rest of your slides are ready. Your manager asks, “Are we on track for tomorrow?” Give your update.

Respond as you would at work. Maximum 60 seconds.
00:00
