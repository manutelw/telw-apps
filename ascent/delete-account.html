ASCENT Account & Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your ASCENT account and associated personal data without reinstalling or signing in to the app.

Submit your request

Enter the email address associated with your ASCENT account. If it matches an account, ASCENT will send a six-digit verification code to that address.

What happens next

Successful email verification creates a deletion request for manual review. Verification does not itself delete your account or data.

What will normally be deleted

After verification and review, deletion normally includes the ASCENT account profile and personal data controlled by ASCENT that is no longer required, including recordings, transcripts, attempts, scores, feedback and progress records, subject to lawful retention needs.

What may be retained

Limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, accounting or payment records, fraud prevention, security, an active dispute, or an institution's legitimate assessment or record-keeping obligations. Where this applies, retention will be limited to the relevant purpose.

Processing: Verified deletion requests are reviewed before any deletion takes place. We may contact you if clarification is needed.

Deleting the account may permanently remove access to paid or assigned ASCENT content associated with that account.
