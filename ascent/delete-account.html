Delete my ASCENT account

You can request deletion without reinstalling or signing in to the ASCENT app.

Submit your request

Email the address below from the email account connected to ASCENT. Include your full name, ASCENT email or student ID, and your institution or private access name.

Request account deletion

If the button does not open your email application, send the request directly to manutelw@gmail.com with the subject ASCENT account deletion request.

What will be deleted

After identity verification, deletion normally includes the ASCENT account profile and personal data associated with it, including recordings, transcripts, attempts, scores, feedback and progress records controlled by ASCENT.

What may be retained

Limited information may be retained where needed for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution or an institution's legitimate assessment and record-keeping obligations. We will explain any applicable retention when responding to the request.

Processing: We aim to acknowledge requests within seven business days and complete verified deletion requests within 30 days, unless a longer period is required for a stated legal or institutional reason.
