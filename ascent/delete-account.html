ASCENT Account & Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your ASCENT account and associated personal data without reinstalling or signing in to the app.

Submit your request

Email us from the email address connected to ASCENT. Include your full name, ASCENT email or student ID, and institution or private-access name.

Request account deletion

If the button does not open your email app, send the request to manu@telw.co.in with the subject ASCENT account deletion request.

What will normally be deleted

After identity verification, deletion normally includes the ASCENT account profile and personal data controlled by ASCENT that is no longer required, including recordings, transcripts, attempts, scores, feedback and progress records, subject to lawful retention needs.

What may be retained

Limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, accounting or payment records, fraud prevention, security, an active dispute, or an institution's legitimate assessment or record-keeping obligations. Where this applies, retention will be limited to the relevant purpose.

Processing: We will acknowledge the request and process verified deletion within the period required by applicable law. We may contact you if identity verification or clarification is needed.

Deleting the account may permanently remove access to paid or assigned ASCENT content associated with that account.
