How the evaluation works

Use the CV by itself for quality review, or add the JD when you need role-specific feedback.

1

Paste the real CV

Start with the document you would actually submit. The evaluator needs the real wording, evidence and structure to diagnose what is strong and what is weakening the profile.

2

Check the CV on its own merits

Stage A looks at CV quality before role fit. This keeps a weak document from hiding behind keyword matching.

3

Add the target JD for role fit

When a JD is available, the evaluator checks eligibility and coverage against the role. This shows whether the CV is merely polished or actually relevant to the opportunity.

Stage B · CV–JD Match
EligibilityBasic requirements
CoverageHow much of the JD is supported
GapsMissing or weak proof
DefenceLikely interview pressure points
Correction + interview defence
Correction point: Replace a vague duty with a result, action and context that you can prove.
Defence question: “What exactly did you do, and what was the outcome?”
4

Revise, then prepare to defend

The useful endpoint is not a prettier CV. It is a stronger document plus a clearer understanding of the claims an interviewer may probe.

Why it is distinct

Quality and role fit are separate questions.

A CV can be well written and still be a poor match for the job. The evaluator checks both.
