How the Live Mock works

The experience is designed to create the uncertainty and follow-up pressure of a real interview.

1

Create or use your Live Mock account

Live Mock has its own sign-in and access. It does not depend on ASCENT learner accounts, access codes or sessions.

2

Check access and start deliberately

The home screen shows access status, attempts remaining and the Start Live Interview control. The aim is to enter the mock as a real attempt, not casual browsing.

3

Answer aloud and handle follow-ups

The interview is spoken. The system can follow up based on what you say, which means a polished first answer is not enough—you have to keep thinking and responding naturally.

4

Review performance after the pressure

The value comes from seeing what breaks down when you must answer live: evidence, structure, judgement, clarity, confidence or follow-up handling.

Why it is distinct

Prepared answers are not the same as interview performance.

Live Mock adds unpredictability, follow-ups and time pressure so you can test whether your preparation survives a real conversation.
