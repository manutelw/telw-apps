How can ASCENT Coach help you?

Use ASCENT Coach to understand your practice and feedback, decide what to work on next, or get help when something in the app is not working.

Questions you can ask
What should I practise next? What is my weakest area? Explain my recent feedback. What am I doing well? What one thing should I improve next? Which category should I focus on? Help me use TELW. Am I improving? Which assigned task should I do next? Help me understand a follow-up question.
Safe repair boundary. ASCENT can retry or refresh learner-level browser problems. It cannot change scores, payments, access, attempts, assignments, question banks, rubrics or shared app data. Problems that need wider changes are recorded for administrator review.
ASCENT Coach is not a general chatbot. Requests about coding, GitHub, duplicating apps, unrelated academic work, general knowledge or other non-ASCENT tasks are outside its scope.
When the Coach will refer you to a human. Payment issues, account-access problems, institute-specific placement rules, disciplinary matters, batch changes, trainer overrides and other decisions requiring human authority must be handled by ASCENT support, your trainer or your institute as applicable.
Important use warningRepeated out-of-scope, abusive or deliberate misuse of ASCENT Coach may result in suspension or termination of your ASCENT access. Charges already paid are non-refundable, except where applicable law or the payment platform requires otherwise.

ASCENT does not provide permanent Coach chat history. If you want to keep the visible conversation, use Download Conversation before leaving this page.
