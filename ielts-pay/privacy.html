Diagnostics: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This policy explains how TELW The English Workshop, operated by Manu Vikraman as sole proprietor, New Delhi 110016, India, handles personal data when you buy or take the TELW IELTS diagnostics at manuvikraman.com/ielts/ or the Communication Performance Diagnostic at manuvikraman.com/diagnostic/. Section 6 sets out what is different for the Communication Performance Diagnostic.

1. What we collect

  • Contact: your email address, used to link your purchase, verify you and send results you ask for.
  • Payment: the product, amount, date and Razorpay order and payment references. Card, UPI and bank details are collected by Razorpay, not by TELW.
  • Test data: your answers, written responses, timings, scores and feedback.
  • Technical data: information needed to run the site securely, such as session cookies and IP addresses used to prevent misuse.

2. Why we use it

  • To provide the diagnostics you bought, mark them and show your results.
  • To process payments, refunds and records required for tax and accounts.
  • To answer your questions and, if you ask for it, to follow up about training with TELW.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse.

3. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help run the diagnostics: website hosting and storage, email delivery, payment processing (Razorpay) and the marking of Writing responses. They may only use it to provide their service to TELW. We do not sell your data. We may disclose data where the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep test data for up to 2 years so that your results and any training follow-up remain available, and payment records for as long as tax law requires. You can ask us to delete your test data sooner.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent for optional emails, by writing to manutelw@gmail.com. We reply within 7 working days.

6. Communication Performance Diagnostic

  • What we collect: your name, email address, optional WhatsApp number, the recordings of your spoken answers, written versions of them, and your report.
  • Who processes it: your recordings are processed by a service provider that helps TELW turn speech into text and prepare your report. TELW may listen to your recordings to check the quality of your report.
  • How long we keep it: everything from your test (recordings, answers, report and contact details) is deleted automatically 7 days after the test. If you pay TELW's full training fee within those 7 days, it is kept for 90 days for your training and then deleted.
  • What we keep longer: a scrambled form of your email address that cannot be read back, the question numbers you have answered and the date of your last test, for one year, so that you get new questions each time and the 7-day gap between tests can be applied.

7. Grievances

Any grievance should be conveyed by a message on WhatsApp to +91 97171 16831.
