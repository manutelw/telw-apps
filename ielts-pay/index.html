About the Communication Performance Diagnostic

Find out how you come across when you speak, and what to fix first.

  • Four short speaking tasks: explain an idea, give your opinion, handle a situation and judge a proposal.
  • After each answer, a follow-up question based on what you said, just like a real interview or meeting.
  • A personal report on how clear, organised and convincing you are, with examples from your own answers and what to practise next. Your trainer checks every report.

₹999 · about 25 minutes · online, on your laptop or phone

How it works

  1. Enter your email address and pay.
  2. Open the test page, choose I've paid and enter the same email address.
  3. Take the test in a quiet room with a working microphone. Your report is ready within two working days.

For adults (18 and over). Start your test within 30 days of payment. You can take the diagnostic once every 7 days, with new questions each time. Everything from your test is deleted 7 days after it, unless you pay for training with TELW within those 7 days. The report is a guide to what to practise, not a certificate.

How the IELTS diagnostics work

  1. Choose a diagnostic, enter your email address and pay.
  2. Open the test page and verify the same email address with the code we send you.
  3. Take the test when you are ready. Your results appear when you finish.

Each purchase covers one test of each skill bought. You can take each skill once every 15 days, so that each result shows real progress rather than familiarity with the test. These diagnostics follow the IELTS format but are not official IELTS tests, and they do not give an official IELTS score.
