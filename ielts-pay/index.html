How it works

  1. Choose a diagnostic, enter your email address and pay.
  2. Open the test page and enter the same email address. IELTS tests also send you a code to confirm it.
  3. Take the test when you are ready. IELTS results appear when you finish; the Communication report is ready within two working days.

The Communication Performance Diagnostic can be taken once every 7 days, with new questions each time. For IELTS, each purchase covers one test of each skill bought. You can take each skill once every 15 days, so that each result shows real progress rather than familiarity with the test. These diagnostics follow the IELTS format but are not official IELTS tests, and they do not give an official IELTS score.
