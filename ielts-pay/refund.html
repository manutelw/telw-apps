IELTS diagnostics: Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This policy applies to the TELW IELTS Reading, Listening and Writing diagnostics sold at manuvikraman.com/ielts/pay/ by TELW The English Workshop, operated by Manu Vikraman as sole proprietor, New Delhi 110016, India.

1. When you get a full refund

  • Technical fault: if a fault on TELW's side stops your test from being taken or marked, and TELW cannot fix it or offer you a replacement test that you accept, you get a full refund for that test.
  • Duplicate payment: if you were charged twice for the same order, the extra payment is refunded in full.

2. When there is no refund

  • Purchases cannot be cancelled once payment is made. Unused tests are not refundable.
  • Once a test has been started, it is not refundable, except for a technical fault on TELW's side.
  • Once any test in a full diagnosis has been started, the full diagnosis is not refundable, except for a technical fault on TELW's side.
  • There is no refund because of the result you received, because you did not finish a test in time, or because the test was taken using a different or incorrect email address that you entered.
  • Unused tests expire 6 months after payment.

3. How to ask for a refund

Email manutelw@gmail.com with the email address you paid with, the Razorpay payment reference and a short description of the problem. We reply within 2 working days.

4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through Razorpay within 7 working days of approval. Your bank or card provider may take a few more days to show the money in your account.
