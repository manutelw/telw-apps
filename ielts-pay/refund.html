IELTS diagnostics: Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This policy applies to the TELW IELTS Reading, Listening and Writing diagnostics sold at manuvikraman.com/ielts/pay/ by TELW The English Workshop, operated by Manu Vikraman as sole proprietor, New Delhi 110016, India.

1. Technical faults: a replacement test

If a fault on TELW's side stops your test from being taken or marked, TELW sends you a replacement test for that skill. A replacement is given instead of a refund.

  • The replacement is added to the email address you used, and its details and a TELW reference number are emailed to you.
  • Faults that the system records itself, such as marking that does not complete, are replaced automatically.
  • For other faults, use the Report a problem form. TELW checks the report against its records and, where the fault is on TELW's side, sends the replacement.
  • Replacement tests are valid for 6 months.

2. Duplicate payment

If you were charged twice for the same order, the extra payment is refunded in full.

3. When there is no refund

  • Purchases cannot be cancelled once payment is made. Unused tests are not refundable.
  • Once a test has been started, it is not refundable.
  • Once any test in a full diagnosis has been started, the full diagnosis is not refundable.
  • There is no refund because of the result you received, because you did not finish a test in time, or because the test was taken using a different or incorrect email address that you entered.
  • Unused tests expire 6 months after payment.

4. How refunds are paid

Refunds for duplicate payments are made to the original payment method through Razorpay within 7 working days of approval. Your bank or card provider may take a few more days to show the money in your account.
