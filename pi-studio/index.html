1. Understand the question

You are identifying the interviewer's intent, not giving your interview answer yet.

2. Plan with TELW

Use the shorter answer when explaining experience. Use the longer answer when explaining decisions and reasoning. If both are needed, the longer answer includes the experience.

3. Build and improve the answer

Use real experience only. If the question is hypothetical, explain what you would do. Do not invent achievements.

4. Speak and retry

Record an answer, listen to it and try again. Audio stays in this browser session; it is not uploaded to our server.

This recording is for self-review only. The AI evaluates typed text, not your audio or pronunciation. Recording requires browser microphone permission.

5. Handle a follow-up

Use the button to generate a follow-up based on your answer.

6. Mock interview and progress

Run a five-question practice set. Answer each question using the earlier stages, then return here to track your progress.

Progress is saved in this browser only. There is no trainer dashboard or cross-device sync in this version.

AI feedback is guidance, not a guaranteed interview outcome. Check borderline feedback with your trainer.
