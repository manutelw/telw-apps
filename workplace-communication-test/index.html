Workplace Communication Test

This assessment measures how well you understand, write and speak in realistic workplace situations. It is not a grammar quiz. Complete it independently, use headphones, and do not refresh the page after you begin.

Candidate details

Before you begin
You will need a working microphone. Your two spoken responses are recorded in your browser and included in the submission package you download at the end.

3. Write for Work

20 marks · evaluator scored

You promised your manager a client-ready report by 4:00 PM. At 3:10 PM you discover that one data source contains errors. The corrected data will be available by 5:00 PM. Write a professional email to your manager explaining the situation, what you are doing, and when the final report can be delivered.

4. Speak to Explain

20 marks · evaluator scored

In 60–90 seconds, explain a project, internship task or academic assignment in which you had to coordinate with other people to complete something. Make clear what the task was, what you personally did, and what happened.

Not recorded

5. Workplace Situation

20 marks · evaluator scored

You are preparing a report due in 45 minutes. At the same time, a colleague tells you that a figure already shared with a senior manager may be wrong. In 60–90 seconds, explain what you would do and why.
