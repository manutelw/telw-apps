TELWSpoken English Lab
Spoken first · English through English

Learn English by using it.

This lab teaches spoken English through listening, elicitation, short oral drills, variation, personalisation and freer conversation. Grammar appears inside use rather than as the starting point.

Unit engine: recall → demonstrate → elicit → controlled speaking → vary → personalise → combine → converse → repair → recycle.
Unit 1

Languages of the World

Talk about languages, approximate numbers, what languages are used for, and why English matters to you. Build useful patterns such as at least, almost, I use it for…, I need it to…, so that…, and simple relative clauses.
